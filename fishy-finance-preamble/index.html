MISSION, VISION & EDITORIAL CHARTER

Preamble

Financial markets make information visible, but visibility does not always mean understanding.

Behind a price movement may be a transaction. Behind a transaction may be a financing arrangement, an ownership structure, a change in control, or a movement of capital that is not immediately apparent from the headline.

FishyFinance exists to look beneath that visible layer.

We investigate the financial structures, transactions, ownership, capital movements, corporate actions and regulatory developments that shape public companies and markets, and we make those structures easier to understand.

Our work is grounded in a simple belief:

The story should never be stronger than the evidence behind it.

1. OUR MISSION

FishyFinance's mission is to investigate the financial mechanics behind market stories and present them with clarity, independence and integrity.

We seek to connect the events that markets see with the financial structures that may sit behind them.

We investigate not only what happened, but, where the available record permits, how it happened, where the capital moved, who was affected, and what the structure reveals.

2. OUR VISION

Our vision is a financial information environment in which investors and readers can look beyond headlines, charts and market narratives and understand the underlying mechanics of the companies and transactions they encounter.

We want complicated financial structures to become understandable without becoming distorted.

We believe better financial understanding begins when the underlying evidence can be seen, followed and questioned.

3. OUR MOTTO

Follow the Movement.

Money moves.

Shares move.

Debt moves.

Ownership moves.

Control moves.

The movement often tells a story that the headline does not.

FishyFinance follows that movement to understand the structure behind the market narrative.

4. OUR EDITORIAL INTEGRITY

Integrity is fundamental to our work.

We distinguish between what is documented, what is reported by an authority, what can reasonably be inferred, and what remains uncertain.

We do not present allegations as established facts, inference as certainty, or suspicion as proof.

Where the available information does not establish an answer, we acknowledge the uncertainty.

Where the evidence changes, our understanding may change with it.

Our commitment is not to a predetermined conclusion.

Our commitment is to the integrity of the record.

5. OUR EVIDENCE STANDARD

FishyFinance values information that can be examined, traced and understood.

We give particular importance to the underlying financial and corporate record, including filings, financial statements, regulatory documents, corporate disclosures and other authoritative records.

Our objective is not simply to tell readers what we believe.

It is to show readers the basis on which an investigation reaches its conclusions.

Evidence comes before narrative.

6. OUR INDEPENDENCE

FishyFinance is independent of the companies, individuals, institutions and market participants that may appear in our investigations.

We do not publish to promote a company, attack a company, support a stock, or manufacture a market narrative.

Our investigations may be favorable, unfavorable, inconclusive or critical depending on what the record establishes.

The conclusion follows the evidence.

7. FAIRNESS AND LEGAL RESPONSIBILITY

FishyFinance recognizes the difference between investigating conduct and declaring guilt.

Unusual activity is not, by itself, evidence of wrongdoing.

A regulatory allegation is not automatically a final finding.

An unusual transaction is not automatically unlawful.

A correlation is not automatically causation.

Where serious allegations or legal matters are involved, we seek to describe the underlying record accurately, distinguish allegation from finding, and avoid presenting unsupported conclusions as fact.

FishyFinance does not act as a court, regulator or prosecutor, and our investigations should not be understood as legal determinations.

8. WHAT WE DO NOT DO

FishyFinance does not:

  • manufacture facts, sources or quotations;

  • create certainty where the record remains uncertain;

  • manufacture controversy for attention;

  • turn ordinary corporate activity into extraordinary allegations;

  • present investment promotion as investigation;

  • publish conclusions that the available record cannot support; or

  • allow a compelling narrative to override material evidence.

We are not a stock-tip service.

We are not a promotional research desk.

We are not a substitute for professional legal, financial or investment advice.

9. OUR COMMITMENT TO READERS

We respect the reader's ability to think.

Our responsibility is not to tell readers what they must conclude.

It is to make complicated financial information sufficiently clear that readers can understand:

what happened, what the record establishes, what can reasonably be inferred, and what remains unknown.

We aim to make financial complexity more accessible without removing the complexity that matters.

10. OUR COMMITMENT TO CORRECTION

No investigation is beyond correction.

If we discover that a published statement is materially wrong, we will seek to correct the record appropriately.

If circumstances change after publication, we will distinguish a new development from an error in the original reporting.

Our objective is not to appear infallible.

It is to remain accountable to the evidence.

11. THE FISHYFINANCE STANDARD

Every investigation ultimately returns to a simple principle:

Look beneath the headline. Follow the movement. Examine the structure. Respect the evidence.

Financial markets may react loudly.

Capital often moves quietly.

FishyFinance exists to follow that movement and make what lies beneath it easier to see.
